# tanisense
TaniSense operates a fleet of autonomous drones that map soil health, moisture, and pest pressure across Javanese rice terraces. Our vision model flags nutrient deficiencies up to two weeks before they are visible to the eye, letting farmers cut pesticide use by 43% while lifting yields by double digits. The Bali pilot covers 1,200 hectares with 84 farmer cooperatives, and the platform now offers a per-hectare subscription that pays for itself within one harvest cycle.
